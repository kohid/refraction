# Builder (Form Builder)

Refraction > Builder lets the site owner design their own record types ("collections") such as Bookings, Listings or Transactions, manage the records in an admin table, and optionally take submissions or show records on the site. Added in 0.6.0; since 0.16.0 every form is built in the page editor (a Form page, see below). Pure-logic tests: `php scripts/test-form-builder.php`; browser checks (each `--site DIR --port N`, serve the site with `php -d upload_max_filesize=20M -d post_max_size=25M -S ...`): `check-form-builder.mjs` (overview, records, public form, listings, delete), `check-form-builder-9.mjs` (Form page, panel, cog, save, site), `check-form-builder-10.mjs` (Steps, Group, Repeater, conditions, uploads, signature) and `check-form-builder-11.mjs` (Choice cards, Badge, Price). Test collections are seeded with `scripts/fixtures/form-builder-seed.php` and the starting points in `scripts/fixtures/form-builder-presets.php`.

## Design reference

The screens follow the layout language of the Metronic 8 admin demo (cards with a header, solid light inputs, soft colored badges, round initials, dashed table rows, pill pagination). Metronic is a paid ThemeForest template, so **no Metronic file, CSS, JavaScript, font or markup is copied or shipped**; the styles in `modules/form-builder/assets/scss/` are written from scratch (`--rfb-*`, `.rfb-*`).

## What it does

- **Collections** (Builder overview): "New form" opens a new Form page in the editor; each collection has "Edit form", records, its shortcodes and a delete card. Stored in the option `refraction_form_builder_collections`.
- **The old designer (0.7.0 to 0.15.0) was removed in 0.16.0.** Its Design screen, "New collection" screen and `builder.js` are gone; collections are made and changed only as Form pages in the page editor. The notes from 0.11.0 to 0.13.0 below describe the Builder field types and rules the editor widgets save into (panels, columns, icon lists, cards), which `Schema` still cleans, validates and stores.
- **Accordion, Tabs and Choice cards (0.11.0)**:
  - **Accordion and Tabs** hold *panels*. A panel is a real field of the hidden type `panel` (not in the field list; add and remove panels from the host's settings or its "Add panel" button) that holds any field, including groups, repeaters and choice cards, but not steps or another accordion or tabs. They have no value: `Schema::flat()` replaces them by their fields, so values are validated, stored, exported and sorted as if the fields sat at the top (or in the group or repeater row around the host). The renderer keeps the same input names. A panel's visibility rule hides and skips every field inside it (server and browser). The accordion uses `<details>` (works without JavaScript; one open at a time unless "several open" is on); tabs are a tab row with arrow keys that appears only when the script runs (without it every panel is shown). On submit a closed panel or inactive tab holding an invalid field opens first, so its message is visible.
  - **Choice cards** (`choice_set`): children are Group or Repeater *cards* (nothing else). Each card has a title, badge, short text, price, period, a note for "no price" and a "What's included" details area. The set is the value: a list of picked cards `[{key, title, price, per}]` (one, or several when "pick several" is on). Title and price are written by the server from the form definition, so a post can not change them; a card that does not exist or is hidden by its rule can not be picked. The fields inside a card are rendered with every control disabled under the name `rfb_locked[...]` and are never read on submit; they can not be required. The classes are `.rfb-choice-card`, `.rfb-cc-*`.
  - **Styling**: each has its own Style groups (accordion panel and header, tab buttons and content, each card, title and price), with Normal / Hover / Open-Selected states.
- **Free layout (0.12.0)**:
  - **Widths**: every field, column and card takes a share of the row: full, 90, 80, 3/4, 70, 2/3, 60, 1/2, 40, 1/3, 30, 1/4, 1/5 and 10 percent (`Schema::WIDTH_PERCENT`, one table; the SCSS and the designer mirror it). A share is the percent of the row minus its share of the gaps, so shares that add up to 100% fill the row exactly. The two older widths (half, third) still stretch when alone on a row; the newer ones keep their width. On screens under 640px everything is full width.
  - **Columns** (`columns`): a host of panels where each panel is a column with a width. Ready-made splits in the settings panel (50/50, 33x3, 25x4, 20x5, 10/90, 20/80, 25/75, 30/70, 40/60 and their mirrors, 25/50/25, 20/60/20); choosing fewer columns moves the fields of the removed ones to the last column. Columns holds fields, groups, repeaters (not uploads in a repeater), choice cards and more Columns, but no Accordion or Tabs, and works inside a group, a repeater row, a panel and a card. Like Accordion and Tabs it stores nothing of its own.
  - **Choice card layout**: the set has `card_columns` (1 to 6) and `card_gap`; a card has `card_width` (auto = an equal share of the set's cards per row, or any width key) and `card_inline` (always show the content instead of "What's included"). A card holds any content block, Columns and non-upload fields (inputs stay locked as before; headings, paragraphs, images, Icon Lists and HTML are plain content). Nesting rules live in one function, `Schema::allowed_in( type, parent, root )` (the old designer mirrored it in `builder.js`).
  - **Icon List** (`icon_list`, a layout block): up to 30 items `{text, icon, link}`; the icon is a name of a bundled icon (`assets/icons/material`, fetched with `scripts/fetch-icons.mjs`; the designer offers `Schema::list_icons()`), the link must be http(s). Vertical or horizontal. Style groups: list gap, icons (color, background, size, hover color), item text (color, size, weight, hover color).
  - **Image style**: the picture has an *Image box* group (alignment and width, written as margins and a percent on `.rfb-image`) and a *picture* group (fit, height, border, radius, shadow, hover); the text options of the old Input group no longer apply to images. The block's own Alignment (in Settings) still works and shows only when the width is below 100%.
- **Free choice cards (0.13.0)**:
  - **Card modes**: a card (a Group or Repeater inside a Choice cards set) is `standard` (the old fixed header: title, badge, short text, price, "What's included") or `free`. A free card is an empty container: a pick control plus any blocks (heading, paragraph, badge, price, image, Icon List, HTML, Columns, Accordion or Tabs, and non-upload fields). Switching a standard card to free puts its title, badge, text and price into blocks (`cardToFree`). What is saved with a record never depends on the blocks: the title is the card's name and the price is the card's own "Price saved with the record" setting (a Price block can show it, or have its own amount).
  - **Pick control**: `marker_pos` (left, right, top-left, top-right, hidden) and `marker_look` (radio, tick, none), set on the set and optionally overridden per card (empty = same as the set). The real radio or checkbox covers the whole card, invisibly, so a click anywhere picks it, keyboard and screen readers work, and a script click handler covers themes that stop the click. Links, toggles and controls inside a card do not pick it.
  - **Styles**: a card is styled as the virtual type `card` (`type_styles.card`, the card's own `style`): the generic box groups (spacing, background, border, shadow, hover) apply to the card element itself, plus its **Selected** look (`cs_*`), the pick control (`mk_*`, `mko_*`) and its size. Selecting a card never edits the Group style.
  - **Blocks**: `badge` (text in a pill) and `price` (the card's price or its own amount, with your currency and period; empty when there is no price). A heading with "Show the card's name" prints the card's name.
- **Forms in the page editor (0.14.0, stage 1)**:
  - **Form page**: document type `rfb-form` (`Documents\Form_Document`, a library document on `refraction_library`), created from Refraction > Builder ("New form in the page editor", `Editor_Forms::new_url()` through the editor's own `refraction_new_post` action) and previewed on the blank canvas. Its container carries `rfb-front`, so the form styles apply in the preview and on the site.
  - **Panel**: the field widgets (`Base\Field_Widget`, one small class per type in `widgets/field-*.php`, names `rfb-field-<type>`, plus `rfb-submit`) live in the `refraction-form-fields` category ("Form Builder"), which `Form_Document::get_editor_panel_categories()` puts first. They only show in the panel on a Form page. There are no form copies of the editor's own widgets: layout and content come from Container, Grid, Heading, Image, Text Editor, Icon List, Accordion, Tabs and the rest.
  - **Field widget**: Content (label, hide label, required, placeholder, choices as `value|Label` lines, lowest / highest, price period, name parts, hidden source, default, description, help), Style (label, input with Normal / Hover / Focus, choices, description and help, all with the editor's typography, border, shadow and responsive controls) and Advanced (the editor's Layout, Motion, Responsive and other sections, plus **Records table**: field key, table column, public, filter). `raw_field()` maps the settings to a Builder field; the markup is `Field_Renderer`'s, so validation and look match the rest of the Builder.
  - **Cog**: the page settings hold the form-wide values (sections Form, Submit and steps, Notifications, Availability and spam, Currency; Style: form colors and sizes as `--rfb-*` variables on the page, so a field's own Style still wins).
  - **Save**: on `refraction/document/after_save`, `Classes\Editor_Forms::sync()` reads the saved elements (`_refraction_data`, not through the document, because opening a new page saves it) in page order, gives each field widget a key once (its own Field key, else the key it had, else one from its label; stored per element id in `_rfb_field_keys`) and saves the collection through `Collections::save()` with the cog values. The page id is kept per collection in the option `refraction_fb_editor_forms`; an empty new page does not become a collection.
  - **Site**: `[refraction_form]` of such a collection prints the editor page inside the form (`get_builder_content_for_display`), and the field widgets print the collection's cleaned fields with the visitor's answers and errors (`Editor_Forms::begin()` / `rendering()`). Without a Submit button widget a plain button is added at the end. The Builder's own designer is not used for these collections ("Edit form" opens the editor).
- **Forms in the page editor, stage 2 (0.15.0)**:
  - **Group and Repeater** (`rfb-field-group`, `rfb-field-repeater`, `Base\Nested_Field` on the editor's `Widget_Nested_Base`) hold one container where you drop fields. On save their fields become the group's or repeater's children (`Editor_Forms::collect()` keeps the nesting); groups and repeaters do not nest inside each other (the Builder rule). On the site the group prints its container inside its fieldset, the repeater prints it once per row and once more as the `__INDEX__` template; while they print, `Editor_Forms::push_scope()` gives the fields inside their name (`values[address][street]`, `values[guests][0][guest_name]`), error path and saved answers.
  - **Steps** (`rfb-steps`): a nested widget with a step repeater (title, description) and one container per step, shown in the editor each under its numbered title. On save each step becomes a step break followed by its fields, so `Schema::steps()` works as before. On the site every step is a `section[data-rfb-step]` inside `[data-rfb-steps]`; the script shows one at a time, puts Back / Next in a row after the steps and, when the Submit button sits in the last step, Back next to it.
  - **Conditional visibility** (Advanced tab of every field, group and repeater): on or off, shown or hidden when all or any rules match; a rule names the Field key of another field, a condition and a value. Saved as the Builder's `conditions` and enforced in the browser and on the server as before.
  - **File upload** (`rfb-field-file`: allowed types, size per file, number of files) and **Signature** (`rfb-field-signature`: text on the pad, pad height).
  - **Live printing**: `Form_Document::print_elements()` skips the page cache and the shortcode turns the element placeholders off while it prints, because fields carry the visitor's answers and errors and get their names from the group or row they are printed in.
  - The editor script `form-builder-editor` (`modules/form-builder/assets/js/editor/`) registers the three nested widgets as nested element types.
- **Forms in the page editor, stage 3 (0.16.0)**:
  - **Choice cards** (`rfb-field-choice_set`, `Widgets\Field_Choice_Set` on `Widget_Nested_Base`): a card repeater (name, price saved with the record, period, width, pick control position and look) and one editor container per card, where you drop any widget (Heading, Image, Badge, Price, Icon List, Accordion, fields...). The set has a label, required, pick several, the card picked at first, cards per row, gap and the pick control; Style has Cards (padding, radius, Normal / Hover / Selected) and Pick control. On save each card becomes a free `card` Group of the set (`Editor_Forms::collect()`), named and priced from the card settings, with the fields of its container as children; the record stores `[{key, title, price, per}]` written by the server.
  - **On the site** each card prints its container inside `.rfb-cc-content` with every input disabled (nothing typed in a card is read), the invisible pick input over the whole card, and `Editor_Forms::push_card()` so a Price inside knows its card.
  - **Badge** (`rfb-badge`: text, alignment, colors, typography, padding, radius) and **Price** (`rfb-price`): in a card it shows the card's own price and period (in the editor it finds the card in the saved page, `Editor_Forms::card_item_of()`), otherwise its own amount and period; written with the form's currency settings. Both sit in the Form Builder category.
  - Forms made in the old designer are not converted (there were none to keep).
- **Amount and the currency (0.11.0)**: the Amount field (`currency`) is a text box with the currency symbol and an optional period (hour, day, week, month, quarter, year or your own text). While typing, `frontend.js` writes thousands and decimals with the collection's separators (1200 -> 1,200; 350329.45 -> 350,329.45), limits the decimals, ignores letters, keeps the caret next to the same digit and checks the lowest and highest values. The **General** tab has a **Currency** card: symbol, position, thousands separator, decimal separator and decimal places (`settings.currency_*`, `thousands_sep`, `decimal_sep`, `decimals`; `Classes\Money`). The same format is used by the table, the public list and the cards. The server parses what was typed with the collection's separators and stores a plain decimal (`1234.50`), so sorting, CSV and rules keep working; visibility rules see the plain number too.
- **Field types**: text, long text, number, amount, email, phone, link, date, time, date and time, dropdown, single choice, multiple choice, yes/no switch, rating, **file upload**, **signature**, **hidden field**, **name**, **country**, **group**, **repeater**, and layout blocks **step**, **heading**, **paragraph**, **image**, **custom HTML** and **divider**. Per field: required, width, placeholder, help, default, options, number limits, CSS class, hide label, "show in table", "add a filter", "show publicly", and **conditional visibility**.
- **Name** (choose prefix, first, middle and last name; a required name needs first and last) and **Country** (ISO list, optional pre-selected country, stored as the code and shown as the name). **Image** (media library picture with size, width, alignment, alt text and link; only the attachment ID is stored). **Signature**: visitors draw on a pad with a mouse, finger or pen; the picture is sent as a PNG, decoded strictly on the server (PNG signature, dimensions, some ink), and stored as a private file like an upload. It needs JavaScript and is not keyboard-accessible, so offer another way to confirm when that matters.
- **Titles and descriptions**: every field and block can have a description under its label. A step, group or repeater title can be shown or hidden on the form (hidden titles stay for screen readers) and is written as `div`, `p` or `h2` to `h6`.
- **Multi-step**: Step fields split the form into pages with Back / Next, a progress display and per-step validation. Without JavaScript every step is shown on one page.
- **Conditional visibility**: show or hide a field when all or any of up to ten rules match (is, is not, contains, does not contain, is empty, is not empty, greater, less). Rules look at other fields, including siblings inside a group or repeater row. A hidden field is never required and never saved; a hidden field counts as empty for rules that follow it. The same rules run in the browser and on the server (the server decides).
- **Group**: fields kept together; their answers are stored under the group. **Repeater**: rows visitors add and remove (minimum and maximum rows, button text); blank rows are dropped.
- **File upload**: allowed extensions per field (jpg, jpeg, png, gif, webp, pdf, doc, docx, xls, xlsx, csv, txt, zip, mp3, mp4), size limit up to 64 MB (the server's own `upload_max_filesize` also applies), up to 10 files. Checked in the browser and again on the server by extension, WordPress's file type check and the real content type. Files are stored outside the media library in `wp-content/uploads/refraction-builder/<collection>/` under a random name; the original name is only a label. Only administrators can download them (through a nonce-protected handler, always as an attachment); deleting a record or collection deletes its files.
- **Hidden field**: a fixed value, a URL parameter, the page address, the referrer, the logged-in user's ID or email, or today's date. It is filled when the page loads, so a visitor can change it: never use it for anything that must be trusted.
- **Records** are posts of the private type `refraction_fb_record` (meta: collection, status, JSON values, one meta key per plain top-level field for sorting and filtering). The table has status tabs with counts, search, dropdown filters, sortable columns, pagination (20 per page), bulk status change, bulk delete and CSV export (group children get their own column, repeater rows and file names are summarised; cells that start with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` get a leading apostrophe).
- **Public form**: `[refraction_form collection="bookings"]`, only when the collection has "Accept submissions from visitors" on. Protected by a nonce, the Advanced tab's spam settings, the opening window and the submission cap. Hook: `refraction/form_builder/submitted` ( $id, $definition, $values ).
- **Public list**: `[refraction_records collection="listings" status="published" view="cards|table" per_page="12"]`, only when "Show records publicly" is on; it shows only the status named in the shortcode and only fields marked "Show publicly" (uploads and hidden fields can not be public). An administrator who mistypes a shortcode sees a hint; visitors see nothing.
- Deleting a collection needs its name typed and removes its records and uploads. Only users who can `manage_options` can use the admin screens; every action has a nonce.

- **Choice looks (0.92.0)**: Choice (one) and Choice (many) fields have a **Look** (`choice_look`): List (as before), Segmented bar, Pill switch, Icon tiles, Cards with text and Image cards. **Full width** (segmented and pill) and **Columns** (tiles, cards and image cards, 0 = as many as fit; on phones cards go one under another and image cards two to a row). Grid tracks use plain fractions, never percentages: in a phone-width editor container, which wraps, a percentage made the field reserve one tile per row of height. **Choice details** (`option_extras`) give a choice a picture (Image cards; a media library id), an icon from the icon libraries, a line of text and a badge; each row names its choice by label or value, is stored keyed by the value, and rows for choices that do not exist are dropped (`Schema::option_extras`). Icons are stored as class names and a library only (no uploaded SVG). The markup keeps the real input in each label with the label text right after it (the Summary reads it); `.rfb-opt-icon`, `.rfb-opt-text` and `.rfb-opt-badge` follow, and the look's CSS places them. Colors are `--rfb-ch-*` variables on the field (`-color`, `-bg`, `-border`, `-icon`, with `-hover-` and `-on-` versions, `-track`, `-track-border`, `-mark`, `-text`, `-radius`, `-gap`, `-icon-size`, `-badge-bg`, `-badge-color`), set from Style > Choices, and fall back on the form's own colors. **Selection mark** (Style > Choices, for tiles, cards and image cards): hide it, Horizontal and Vertical Orientation each with an offset (`--rfb-mark-l/r/t/b`, `--rfb-mark-x/y`), and size; tiles default to a dot at the top left, image cards to a check badge at the top right of the picture, cards keep their radio in the row (the horizontal orientation puts it at either end, the vertical one aligns it, the offsets nudge it). Image cards also have a small radio beside the name (it can be hidden) and a picture shape (4:3 by default). Keyboard focus shows a ring on the option; reduced motion turns off the transitions. Check: `scripts/check-choice-looks.mjs`.

- **Field extras (0.93.0)**:
  - **Icon in the box** (`icon`, `Schema::ICON_TYPES`: text, long text, email, phone, website, number, date, time, datetime, dropdown): an icon from the icon libraries (class names and a library only), printed as `.rfb-field-icon` inside `.rfb-icon-box` around the box, which gets room on the left. A number with minus and plus buttons shows none. Style > Icon in the box: color, color while typing, size, space from the edge, space before the text (`--rfb-icon-*`).
  - **Phone format** (`phone_format`: '' / national / international, `phone_pattern`): `#` is a digit; brackets, spaces, dashes, dots and a leading + are written as the digits reach them (`initPhone` in frontend.js). Backspace over a separator removes the digit before it; the caret stays after the same digit; extra digits are not accepted (maxlength = the pattern's length). A pattern with letters, digits or markup falls back on the format's own (`Schema::phone_pattern`). The server keeps the number as sent (the usual phone check).
  - **Long text grows** (`autogrow`): `data-rfb-autogrow`; the box grows to its content, up to Content > Tallest (max-height), then scrolls.
  - **Country flag** (`country_flag`, on unless switched off, so older forms get it): `.rfb-flag-box` with the chosen country's SVG from `assets/lib/flag-icons/4x3/` (flag-icons 7.2.3, MIT, see NOTICE); a `<select>` can not hold pictures, so the flag sits at the left of the box and follows the choice. Emoji flags were not used: Windows shows them as two letters.
  - **Name parts**: Style > Name parts > Space between First Name and Last Name (`column-gap` of `.rfb-name-row`) and Space when they wrap.
  - Check: `scripts/check-field-extras.mjs`.

## Limits

- Building a form needs the page editor (JavaScript). The public form works without it (all steps on one page, server validation).
- **Upload folder protection**: the folder holds an `.htaccess` and `index.php`. Apache honours the first; nginx does not read `.htaccess`, so on nginx add `location ~* /uploads/refraction-builder/ { deny all; }`. Names are random 32-character values, so they can not be guessed, but a deny rule is the proper protection.
- Signatures inside a repeater are not supported (like uploads). A refused form submission asks the visitor to sign again.
- Uploads inside a repeater are not supported (use a group or a top-level upload).
- Hidden fields can be changed by the visitor; webhooks and email run when the page is submitted, not queued, and are not retried.
- Public form pages should not be fully page-cached (the nonce and minimum-time token are per page load); exclude them from caching plugins.
- Sorting and filtering rely on post meta, which is fine for thousands of records, not millions; only plain top-level fields can be sorted or filtered.
- Conditions can not look at an upload; a group inside a repeater and a repeater inside a repeater are not possible.
- Tested in Chromium on a classic and a block theme, SQLite test site, with PHP's built-in server; not on MySQL, Apache or nginx.
- **Showing a form on any page (0.32.0)**:
  - **Form Canvas** (`rfb-form-canvas`, `widgets/form-canvas.php`): in the Form Builder category on every page except a Form page. Content: the form (all collections; "(not accepting submissions)" marks one with that switch off), a title with its tag and a text above the form. Style: Canvas (width, alignment, padding, background, border, corners, shadow), Title and text, and **Form colors on this page** (main color, button text, labels, field background, border and text, form background, corners), written as `--rfb-*` overrides with `!important` on the form box and the Form page's own container, because the form writes its colors inline. The form itself is `Module::form_html()`, the same output as `[refraction_form]`; the widget is never kept in the element cache.
  - **Shortcode widget**: "Form Builder form" (`rfb_form`, added by the module through `refraction/element/shortcode/section_shortcode/before_section_end`) prints the chosen form below the shortcode, through the new `refraction/widgets/shortcode/content` filter (the widget now prints when only a form is chosen).
  - **The Form page's own address**: `Form_Document::print_elements()` prints the real form (the shortcode's output) when the page is shown on the site by itself, outside the editor and its preview; the shortcode prints the page with `get_builder_content()` there, because the builder's recursion guard returns nothing for the page being viewed.
  - **Page caches**: the form carries `data-rfb-issued` and `data-rfb-token-url`; when the page is older than five minutes, `frontend.js` fetches a fresh nonce and time token from `admin-ajax.php?action=refraction_fb_token` (only for a collection that takes submissions) and a submit made before it arrives waits for it. The script also starts when it is loaded after `DOMContentLoaded`.
  - Check: `scripts/check-form-canvas.mjs`.
- **Booking fields and the taxi booking starter (0.90.0)**:
  - **Total** (`total`, widget `rfb-field-total`): an amount worked out from the answers. **Formula** uses field keys in braces, numbers, `+ - * /`, brackets and `min max round ceil floor abs` (`Classes\Formula`, a small parser with no `eval`, mirrored in `frontend.js`). A field counts as its number; a select, radio or checkbox counts the **number of the chosen choice(s)**, written `value|Label|number`; a set of choice cards counts the prices of the picked cards; a Yes / No switch counts 1 when on. **Other formulas when...** are rules of one or two conditions each (the Conditions engine); the first that matches gives the formula, and an empty formula means no price, shown as the **Text when there is no price**. The script shows the total live; the server never reads a posted total and works it out in `Schema::validate()` (`totals()`) once every answer is known. Only top-level totals and those in Accordion, Tabs or Columns are worked out.
  - **Choice cards**: each card has a **Card key** (stable keys for rules, totals and prefill) and **Offer this card only when** (up to two rules, such as `passengers is less than 5`). These are saved as the card group's `conditions`, so the server also refuses a hidden card.
  - **Date limits** (Date, Date and time): **Notice needed (hours)** (the browser's `min` and a server check), **Bookable up to (days ahead)** (`max`), **Closed on** weekdays (the script's own message, checked on the server). **Time limits** (Time, Date and time): **From**, **Until** and **Minutes between choices** (`step`). "Now" is the site's time zone (`Schema::now()`, which tests set).
  - **Number**: **Minus and plus buttons** (`.rfb-count`, whole steps within the lowest and highest). **Text**: **Suggestions while typing** from your own list (a `<datalist>`, no outside service).
  - **Summary** (`rfb-summary`): the answers given so far as label and value, filled by the script (choices by their labels, cards by their names, the total as shown). Hidden fields, locked card inputs and uploads are left out. Nothing is stored.
  - **Reference** (cog > Notifications > **Reference prefix**): `PREFIX-yymmdd-NNNN`, unique per collection (record meta `_rfb_reference`). It is passed back as `rfb_ref` (only a well-formed one is shown), replaces `{reference}` in the message after sending, and is added to the office email and the webhook (`reference`).
  - **Email the visitor a copy**: to the address in a field, with a subject and a plain-text message using `{reference}`, `{summary}` (every answer, one per line) and `{field_key}`. Replies go to the first notification address. Amounts and totals are written with the form's currency.
  - **Prefill**: Form Canvas > **Answers filled in on this page** (field key = value) becomes the shortcode's `fill="key=value;key=value"`; `?fill_key=value` in the address does the same. Only top-level fields. Choice cards and checkboxes take keys or values separated by commas. Visitors can change them, and the server checks them like any answer.
  - **Starters** (`Classes\Starters`, files in `modules/form-builder/starters/`): Refraction > Builder > **Start from a ready-made form** makes a Form page from the starter's elements and settings, saves it once (which makes the collection) and opens it in the editor. **Taxi and tour booking** (`taxi-booking.php`) has three steps:
